02

How the instrument was built

From the Gold Plans to a statistical model trained on 10 million minutes of observed care.

Timeline

1989 · 1994

Gold Plan, then New Gold Plan

Tax-funded expansion of LTC services. Municipalities get a new role: planning services for older people.

Tax-based welfare
1995

The national time study

51 recommended "high-quality" LTC institutions. Surveyors shadowed 2,376 caregivers minute by minute for 48 hours: about 10 million minutes of care to 3,800 seniors, coded into 328 care activities.

Data collection
Until 1996

RUG-III considered, then dropped

The US nursing-home case-mix system did not explain Japanese resource use, and it needed specially trained nurses Japan did not have.

Rejected option
1996 – 1998

National model programmes

Tree-regression models pilot-tested on 175,129 seniors in all municipalities. Agreement with professionals' judgment: 71.5% (1996), 75.3% (1997). Item interrater reliability 0.9 (1997).

Validation
April 2000

LTCI launches

Certified beneficiaries rise from 2 million (2000) to 3.5 million (December 2003).

Implementation
2003

First revision

Fee schedules cut (especially institutional), raised for care management, home help and rehabilitation. A dementia indicator is added to the computer model.

Revision
2006 (planned)

Next revision

Government considering lowering the premium-paying age from 40 to 20. By law, the assessment model is revised every 5 years.

Outlook

Three design principles

1
Reproducible

Any trained investigator using the instrument should get sufficiently similar results for the same applicant.

2
Questionnaire-only

Results are based strictly on the responses to the assessment questionnaire.

3
One standard unit

Need is expressed in a standardized unit: estimated minutes of care per day.

🔑
The big shift

Income, assets and family care availability are no longer relevant to eligibility. LTC became a uniform entitlement based only on physical and mental status.

Inside the time study

The government's goal was to make the care of "ideal" facilities available at home, so the study deliberately sampled facilities recommended by national associations as high quality. Each surveyor was a trained professional from the same institution, which reduced unrecorded care.

⚠️
What was left out, on purpose

Time-study data on home care (by professionals and families) were also collected but not used, because home care use depended mostly on family and housing factors. The model therefore estimates institutional care minutes from body and mind alone.
