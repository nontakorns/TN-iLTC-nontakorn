# Asian LTC eligibility and care-level instruments

## Which Asian primary studies are useful seeds for Q1+Q2?

### Takeaway
The most directly useful Asian families are Japan's LTC certification, Japan's disability care-service allocation, Korea's LTCI assessment-domain validation, and Shanghai's unified needs assessment. Institutional care-level instruments can broaden all-age domains, but they do not establish community entitlement or readiness to leave IMC. Sources below were checked on 12 October 2026; this was a targeted evidence search, not a systematic search.

### Cited Findings

1. **Tsutsui T, Muramatsu N. Care-Needs Certification in the Long-Term Care Insurance System of Japan. J Am Geriatr Soc. 2005;53(3):522–527. DOI 10.1111/j.1532-5415.2005.53175.x; PMID 15743300.** Priority: core historical development seed, with primary/system-description reporting. **Abstract and reference list accessed; not full text.** National initial assessment derived from professional caregiver time studies in LTC institutions and subsequently tested in 175,129 older participants in national model programs. The historical 85-item algorithm assigned seven needs levels; professional board reviewed the initial result. Abstract gives no sensitivity/specificity or independent external-validation statistic. Important Thai lesson: examine development reference standard and separation of algorithm from committee judgment; do not equate national implementation with demonstrated accuracy. Historical tool version must be distinguished from later instruments. [Publisher primary record](https://agsjournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1532-5415.2005.53175.x). Reference-list leads for further retrieval: Seki Y, Tsutsui T, Miyano T. Nihon Ouyou Toukei Gakkaishi. 2000;29:101–110 (statistical-model validity); Tsutsui T. Jap J Soc Welfare. 1997;38:36–52 (certification in 60 regions). Exact English titles/DOIs not verified.

2. **Takei T, Takahashi H, Nakatani H. Developing a uniformed assessment tool to evaluate care service needs for disabled persons in Japan. Health Policy. 2008;86(2–3):373–380. DOI 10.1016/j.healthpol.2007.12.001; PMID 18191276.** Priority: strong all-age-adjacent development seed. **Abstract only (primary PubMed indexed text).** Development dataset: 1,423 care-service recipients across 60 municipalities, including physical, intellectual and mental disabilities. Factor/regression analyses used 106 items; IADL emerged as important for disability levels. Tool guided types and quantities of services nationally. Exact age range, dependent/reference variable, validation split and predictive performance not exposed in abstract; MeSH includes adolescent/adult/middle-aged/aged, but do not infer pediatric validation from indexing. Thai relevance: a separate disability-support system can contain care-needs evidence missed by searches restricted to older people or the term LTC. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18191276/).

3. **Kim YH, Kwon CH, Shin HI. Validity of Motor Impairment Scale in Long-Term Care Insurance System of Korea. Ann Rehabil Med. 2013;37(3):403–412. DOI 10.5535/arm.2013.37.3.403; PMID 23869339; PMCID PMC3713298.** Priority: core concurrent validation of one certification component, not whole-system validation. **Full text accessed.** Cross-sectional analysis of 407 non-dementia older LTCI users: 121 facility, 286 domiciliary/day/night/nursing-service users. Upper/lower limb MIS compared with observed ADL service time, ADL scores and preassigned care grade. Facility correlations with service time were rho=.41/.40; no significant relationship in domiciliary users. Regression explained service-time variance much more poorly at home (adjusted R2=.024 vs .33). Excluding dementia limits coverage; delivered care time reflects supply and informal caregiving, not necessarily normative need. Critical Thai relevance: facility-derived models may fail at home; separate setting validation is essential. [Full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3713298/).

4. **Tsugihashi Y, Hirose M, Iida H, Hayashi S, Yasunaka M, Shirahige Y, Kurita N; ZEVIOUS group. Validating care-needs level against self-reported measures of functioning, disability and sarcopenia among Japanese patients receiving home medical care: The Zaitaku Evaluative Initiatives and Outcome Study. Geriatr Gerontol Int. 2021;21(2):229–237. DOI 10.1111/ggi.14124; PMID 33393206.** Priority: useful current-level construct validation in home care, not certification cutoff accuracy. **Abstract only.** Multicenter cross-sectional study, 181 home medical-care patients able to complete questionnaires. LTC care level correlated moderately with WHODAS-12 (rho=.58) and SARC-F (.44); substantial variation remained within levels. No independent gold-standard eligibility decision. Selection excludes people unable to respond, relevant to cognitive/communication impairment. Useful for testing whether grades reflect patient-perceived disability, but does not establish an IMC discharge threshold. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33393206/).

5. **Cao Yifan, Xue Jia, Wan Lingshan, Wang Changying, Xie Chunyan, Chen Duo, Ding Hansheng. 上海市老年照护统一需求评估标准的框架、特点与启示 [Shanghai unified needs assessment standard: framework, characteristics and implications; English gloss]. Chinese Health Resources. 2022;25(1):63–67. DOI 10.13688/j.cnki.chr.2022.210949.** Priority: development/system-description seed; judge empirical-study eligibility in full screening. **Chinese full text accessed.** Standard formed through expert consultations and longitudinal data: annually >8,000 community older people in one selected district/street, and >10,000 people in 20 nursing and 20 elder-care institutions followed 2014–2021. Models include Bayesian classifiers, neural networks and SVM; data inform grading and revisions. Domains include ADL/IADL, cognition, diseases, mental/emotional status, family care capacity, home environment and recent medical/rehabilitation treatment. Independent reference criterion, train/test performance and reproducible algorithm not reported clearly; weights deliberately hidden. Thai lesson: broad domains and cross-setting data useful; claims of fairness or transferability need independent evidence. [Primary full text](https://html.rhhz.net/ZGWSZY/html/2022-1-63.htm).

6. **Wan Lingshan, Zhang Yunwei, Xue Jia, Cao Yifan, Ding Hansheng. Research on the consistency between the national assessment standards for disability grade in long-term care and the unified assessment standards for elderly care needs in Shanghai. Health Development and Policy Research. 2024;27(2):85–90. DOI 10.12458/HDPR.202405009.** Priority: core comparison of actual grading systems. **Primary abstract/Chinese main-page text accessed; PDF not inspected.** Reclassified 179,141 Shanghai LTCI assessments from January–May 2023 under national versus Shanghai rules. Domains compared: ADL, cognition, perception/communication. ADL/perception distributions were broadly similar, cognition differed; Shanghai grades concentrated in mild/moderate while national grades in moderate/severe disability. No independent gold standard, eligibility outcome or kappa statistic verified. Useful warning: near-similar component scores can still alter severity/entitlement. Not an external gold-standard validation. [Primary journal record](https://hdpr.shdrc.org/CN/abstract/article/2097-4205/3772).

7. **Sun Jiayao, Ma Chunhua, Lü Jun, Tang Qi, Shao Zhimin, Hu Kangning, Huang Haoying, Huang Aihua. Development of a needs assessment tool for grading care services for adults with disabilities in welfare institutions. Chinese Journal of Rehabilitation Theory and Practice. 2025;31(7):802–811. DOI 10.3969/j.issn.1006-9771.2025.07.007.** Priority: all-age-adjacent institutional development seed. **Primary indexed abstract and tables accessed; complete full-text access failed.** At one Shanghai disability institution: n=340 psychometrics, n=1,046 clustering for five care levels. Four domains cover physiological disease/function, ADL, mental/psychological function, environmental adaptation; 43 indicators. Alpha=.956, test–retest=.997, item CVI=.83–1.00. Factor structure/cluster scores assessed, not independently adjudicated entitlement. High internal consistency does not validate thresholds. Thai use: younger disability/cognitive/communication domains; limited community transferability. [Primary journal](https://www.cjrtponline.com/EN/10.3969/j.issn.1006-9771.2025.07.007).

8. **Tang Qi, Lü Jun, Ma Chunhua, Huang Haoying, Sun Jiayao, Tao Ran, Lu Chunlei, Huang Aihua. Application evaluation of the care-need grading assessment tool for adult with disabilities in welfare institutions. Chinese Journal of Rehabilitation Theory and Practice. 2026;32(8):946–958. DOI 10.3969/j.issn.1006-9771.2026.08.008. Published 25 August 2026.** Priority: companion application study to Sun 2025, not independent instrument. **Primary indexed abstract/tables accessed; full-text open failed.** n=1,002 adults in four Shanghai disability care institutions, June–July 2025; mean age 31.75±12.29. Five grades distinguished functional dimensions and associated with external variables (age/disability type/comorbidity); median assessment 5.80 minutes. No accuracy versus independent care entitlement/adjudication. Apparent separation using scores that define clusters risks circular validation. Useful evidence of feasibility in working-age disability, but no proof of IMC-to-LTC transition or home-care suitability. Possible development/application sample overlap requires full-text check. [Primary journal](https://www.cjrtponline.com/EN/10.3969/j.issn.1006-9771.2026.08.008).

### Inferences
- For a small workshop set, prioritize Tsutsui 2005, Takei 2008, Kim 2013, Wan 2024, and Tsugihashi 2021. Add Cao 2022 for development and Sun/Tang for working-age disability if the team includes care-level allocation instruments. This recommendation is a relevance judgment, not an inclusion decision.
- Separate whole-system entitlement, subscale measurement, patient disability construct validation, and care-level allocation in extraction. Most identified evidence tests construct or delivered resources rather than an independent eligibility reference standard.
- Treat observed care minutes cautiously in Thailand: home-service supply, family caregiving, travel and visit schedules could make delivered minutes a poor measure of true need, illustrated empirically by Kim 2013.

### Gaps
- Full historical statistical development/validation reports behind Japanese certification require Japanese-language retrieval; Tsutsui 2005 primarily describes a program and its development rather than exposing the complete empirical analysis.
- No Asian seed above establishes readiness to finish IMC, irreversible disability, rehabilitation plateau, or the correct timing of reassessment. These belong to the transition evidence branch.
- No verified pediatric entitlement-validation study found in this targeted search. Takei's exact age range must be checked; adolescent MeSH is not evidence of an appropriate pediatric instrument.

## Which rules and broader care-needs evidence live outside core validation studies?

### Takeaway
Policy/manual evidence is necessary for actual eligibility rules, age restrictions, setting choices and changing instrument versions. Retain it as a separate evidence stream if the team agrees; do not label policy thresholds validated because a component scale is reliable.

### Cited Findings
- **Japan current-version safeguards:** MHLW's certification page links the investigator's 2009 instrument with April 2024 revision and committee guidance revised April 2021. Official texts refer to 74 assessed items plus physician statement, whereas the 2005 development description uses 85 items. A September 2026 municipal official page confirms >=65 applicants and 40–64 applicants with one of 16 specified diseases; this is not an all-age entitlement model. [MHLW certification manuals](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/stf/seisakunitsuite/bunya/hukushi_kaigo/kaigo_koureisha/nintei/index.html), [MHLW official instruction](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/web/t_doc?dataId=00tb6469&dataType=1), [Inagi City updated 14 September 2026](https://www.city.inagi.tokyo.jp/en/kenko/kaigohoken/1003335/1011857.html).
- **Japan disability support is a separate track:** MHLW disability-support classification page provides national assessment, committee and rare-disease manuals (including April 2025 rare-disease guidance). Official English policy summary says support classification for nursing-care benefits is required only at >=18. Pediatric service policies have separate current Child and Family Agency guidance. [MHLW disability classification](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/stf/seisakunitsuite/bunya/hukushi_kaigo/shougaishahukushi/kubun/index.html), [Official English policy summary](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/wp/hakusyo/kousei/23-2/dl/09_en.pdf), [Pediatric policy](https://www.cfa.go.jp/policies/shougaijishien/shisaku).
- **Taiwan care-management domains:** MOHW reported development beginning in 2011 using feedback from 17,000 assessment users, disability experts and five-city pilot. Six domains: ADL/IADL, communication, complex nursing, cognition/mood/behavior, home/family/social support, caregiver burden. Primary ministry development statement is 2016 and assessment revisions are on official 2019/2022 page; not a claim that this is the complete current 2026 instrument or eligibility policy. [MOHW development](https://www.mohw.gov.tw/fp-2633-10309-1.html), [Official revised form](https://1966.gov.tw/LTC/cp-6440-47563-207.html).
- **Singapore residential rule differs from home service rule:** AIC nursing-home page specifies physical/mental disability, assistance/supervision with mobility/ADLs, and inability to be cared for at home after other home/day options tried; subsidy financial/citizenship conditions are separate. MOH's 8 September 2026 answer says Enhanced Home Personal Care assesses both recipient and caregiving situation using interRAI Check-Up plus professional judgment. Do not infer nursing-home eligibility from home-service assessment. [AIC nursing homes](https://www.aic.sg/Care-Services/Nursing-Home), [MOH HPC+ 2026](https://www.moh.gov.sg/newsroom/eligibility-and-priority-assessments-for-enhanced-home-personal-care-programme/).
- **Singapore non-geriatric tool:** AIC Home Caregiving Grant says Client Assessment Form can assess ADL support in intellectual disability/autism at >=16 for AIC LTC schemes. This is a policy/tool lead, no local criterion-validation paper retrieved. [AIC HCG FAQ](https://www.aic.sg/Financial-Assistance/Home-Caregiving-Grant).
- **Thailand context primary research:** Pholpark A, Srithamrongsawat S. Purchasing Community-Based Long-Term Care Services Under Thailand’s Universal Coverage Scheme: A Provider’s Perspective. Inquiry. 2026. DOI 10.1177/00469580261463906. **Full text accessed.** Qualitative case study with 40 providers/financing stakeholders, 11 Nakhon Pathom subdistricts, October 2023–March 2024. Describes Barthel <=11 program gate and historical service packages, but does not validate it against care need or outcome, nor establish current all-age regulation. Useful for feasibility/provider survey context. [Primary full text](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.1177/00469580261463906).
- **Taiwan ancillary measurement seed:** Lin PS, Lin TC, Chen CC, Chi SF, Chen CK, Wong AMK. An Simplified Comprehensive Assessment Tool for Long-term Care Residents in Taiwan. Rehabilitation Practice and Science. 2014;42(1):41–50. DOI 10.6315/2014.42(1)05. **Abstract only.** PRABC, n=90 residents >=65 from a Taoyuan facility, compared with MDS/CPS/ADL and care time in only 15 people. Interrater ICC=.575, retest=.969; care-time r=.670. Institutional resource/needs assessment, not statutory eligibility. [Primary record](https://rps.researchcommons.org/journal/vol42/iss1/5/).
- **Adjacent but not core:** Yi JJ, Yi SW, Kim JI, Yu SH, Yoo HS. A Relationship of Care Time with Functional Status and Patients Characteristics among Patients in Long-term Care Hospitals. J Prev Med Public Health. 2004;37(3):282–291. PMID 25175475. n=1,001 in eight Korean hospitals; RAI and self-reported staff task survey. Resource/case-mix association study, not eligibility algorithm validation. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25175475/).
- **Taiwan need-profile retrieval lead:** Liu LF, Yao HP. Examining the need assessment process by identifying the need profiles of elderly care recipients in the Ten-year Long-Term Care Project (TLTCP) of Taiwan. J Am Med Dir Assoc. 2014;15(12):946–954. DOI 10.1016/j.jamda.2014.07.007; PMID 25244959. Primary record confirms citation; not extracted fully, so no sample/performance claims. It profiles recipients rather than validating entitlement thresholds. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25244959/).

### Inferences
- Team decision: allow primary disability-service allocation instruments within Q1 when they operationalize sustained care need, or restrict them to contextual evidence. Broad all-age scope otherwise risks losing the very evidence relevant to working-age disability.
- Team decision: policy/manual extraction may need a separate structured lane for gate/age/duration/exclusion/reassessment/appeal/service-setting rules. A validation-only SR cannot recover all actual eligibility criteria.
- Do not import Japanese, Chinese or Singapore thresholds into a Thai prototype. Domains, workflow, setting validation and handling cognition/caregivers are more transferable than foreign cutoff numbers.

### Gaps
- Current Thai primary legal/manual eligibility wording not verified in this branch; an official NHSO 2026 LTC monitoring e-book surfaced but was not extracted. Do not state that all-age eligibility began in 2016 based only on the 2026 qualitative paper's introduction. [Official NHSO lead](https://media.nhso.go.th/view/1/%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%A2%E0%B8%A5%E0%B8%B0%E0%B9%80%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%B5%E0%B8%A2%E0%B8%94_ebook/TH-TH?ebookid=416).
- Current Korean whole-system instrument and grade reform not independently verified; the 2013 paper's three-grade description is historical and must not be described as current.
- Shanghai national policy revisions after the 2021 standards and local current 2026 gates need official policy retrieval. Wan 2024 is a comparison of specified versions.
- No reliable local validation of Singapore Client Assessment Form or policy-specific Check-Up decision cutoffs retrieved.

## How was this branch searched and what remains unresolved?

### Takeaway
Targeted searching retrieved direct development/validation families and exposed age and setting gaps. It does not support estimates of total relevant literature or completeness.

### Cited Findings
- Important exclusion trap: Japanese cohort risk-factor literature generally predicts **future certification**, using certification as functional-disability outcome; this does not validate the certification instrument itself. The 2022 scoping review is useful for finding these articles but is not a primary eligibility-validation seed. [Review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8872097/).
- Pediatric official programs exist in Japan, but developmental support/residential services are separated from adult disability and older-person LTC certification; that makes an older-only LTC search insufficient for the agreed all-age question. [Child and Family Agency](https://www.cfa.go.jp/policies/shougaijishien/shisaku).

### Inferences
- Suggested team discussion: classify validation evidence by target (care-needs construct, reference care minutes, committee certification, later service entry, later health outcome). These have different meanings and should not be pooled as equivalent evidence of eligibility.
- Language expansion beyond English is required for Japanese development reports and Chinese/Taiwan official grading systems; translation must preserve version, score direction and level names.
